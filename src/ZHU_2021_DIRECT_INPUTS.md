# Zhu 2021 inputs used directly in this folder

Paper: X. Zhu, C. Zhang, P. Feng et al., Waste Management 131 (2021) 20–30.

Directly transcribed size distribution from Fig. 3(a):

- >2 mm: 4.18%
- 2–1.5 mm: 18.23%
- 1.5–1 mm: 19.11%
- 1–0.5 mm: 7.47%
- 0.5–0.25 mm: 0.68%
- <0.25 mm: 50.33%

The paper combines 0.25–0.5 and 0.5–1 mm for subsequent pneumatic experiments.

Directly reported pneumatic size classes:

- 2–1.5 mm
- 1.5–1 mm
- 1–0.25 mm

Directly reported size-specific separation air volumes in the mass-balance and
conclusion:

- 2–1.5 mm: 89 m3/h
- 1.5–1 mm: 84 m3/h
- 1–0.25 mm: 75 m3/h

The paper explicitly notes that mixing coarse and fine particles causes:
- small Cu to mix with the Al product;
- large Al to mix with the Cu product.

The paper also states that recovered light products can be separated in cycles
to improve copper recovery and aluminum grade.

Not direct Zhu inputs:
- within-bin continuous size PDF (uniform interpolation is used);
- Stage-1 1.35 m/s project setting;
- pulse frequency/duty/low-flow ratio;
- Stage-3 choice of lower tested airflow point;
- cross-source material composition and liberation distributions already
  present in the original project.
